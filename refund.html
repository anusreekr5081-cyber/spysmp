Refund Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Effective immediately

Thank you for supporting NEXA NETWORK. We operate transparently and want all community members to understand our policy regarding virtual ranks, cosmetics, and donations.

1. Intangible Digital Goods

All ranks, cosmetics, particle effects, and virtual perks offered on NEXA NETWORK are intangible digital goods delivered electronically and immediately upon purchase. Because these virtual items are consumed or activated immediately upon purchase, all sales are considered final except under the specific circumstances outlined below.

2. When Refunds Are Granted

We are happy to issue a full refund under the following conditions:

  • Non-Delivery of Virtual Goods: If you purchased a rank or cosmetic and a technical glitch prevented delivery to your Minecraft account, and our technical support is unable to resolve the issue within 72 hours of your initial ticket.
  • Duplicate Billing: If our payment gateway accidentally processed multiple charges for a single intended order.
  • Fraudulent / Unauthorized Transactions: If an unauthorized party used your payment method, subject to prompt notification and verification.

3. Ineligible Circumstances

Refunds will not be granted under any of the following circumstances:

  • Account suspensions or permanent bans resulting from violations of our server rules (e.g. cheating, x-ray, griefing, harassment, or hate speech).
  • Change of mind after ranks or perks have been claimed, utilized, or displayed in-game.
  • Purchases made by minors without parental permission if the virtual items were already delivered and utilized.

4. Chargebacks & Dispute Policy

Please reach out to our team before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Unauthorized chargebacks or payment disputes result in an automatic, permanent network-wide ban of all linked usernames and IP addresses.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or resolve a transaction dispute, please contact our management team with your transaction ID, Minecraft username, date of purchase, and reason:

  • Management & Support: Instagram @jinshi_1
  • Discord Support Ticket: Open a billing ticket in our official community Discord.

Eligible refund requests are reviewed within 48 hours and typically credited back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
