Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Effective immediately

At NEXA NETWORK ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, protect, and process data when you visit our website (spysmp.live) and use our community services.

1. Minimal Data Collection Principle

We believe in strict data minimization. We do not require or collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or payment card details to create an account. When you register an account, we collect strictly and exclusively:

  • Username: A 3–20 character handle chosen by you to identify your account and community votes.
  • Password: Stored strictly as a one-way cryptographically salted hash. Plaintext passwords are never stored on our disks or accessible to administrators.
  • IP Address: Logged automatically during HTTP requests solely for automated rate-limiting, brute-force mitigation, DDoS defense, and session security.

2. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Strict Protection for Children Under 13: NEXA NETWORK complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

Prior to creating an account, an age verification check is presented. If a user states they are under 13, account creation is strictly blocked and no personal information or account credentials are recorded or saved to our servers. Under-13 visitors may still freely browse public content without registering.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has registered without parental consent, contact our lead administrator immediately on Instagram at @jinshi_1 to have the account permanently deleted.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

We display third-party advertisements via Google AdSense to support server infrastructure costs:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard HTTP cookies strictly for authentication session tokens (identifying your logged-in session securely). We use browser LocalStorage solely to remember your UI preferences (dark/light theme and mascot display state). No tracking or profiling identifiers are stored in our first-party cookies.

5. Security & Data Retention

We maintain industry-standard security safeguards. Passwords are encrypted using state-of-the-art cryptographic hashing. Server communication is encrypted end-to-end via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Account records are retained as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.

6. Data Deletion & Inquiries

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your username and associated vote history at any time. For privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or support, please reach out directly:

  • Lead Administrator: Instagram @jinshi_1
  • Community Discord: Available via our official site links
