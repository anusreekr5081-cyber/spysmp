Community Standards

Five Core Principles.

Transparent, impartial, and uniformly enforced across all servers.

  1. 01

    Mutual Respect

    Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, discrimination, targeting, or toxicity. Treat every community member with professional dignity.

  2. 02

    Fair Competition

    Disallowed client modifications, automated macros, x-ray packs, and exploit abuse result in immediate network disqualification.

  3. 03

    Account Integrity

    Real-world trading, phishing, account sharing to bypass penalties, unauthorized advertising, or chargeback fraud are strictly prohibited.

    Property Protection

    In claim-protected SMP and creative plots, destroying or stealing other players' documented work carries permanent restitution penalties.

  5. 05

    Staff & Appeals Process

    Staff directives must be respected during active moderation incidents. Formal dispute appeals must be logged via official Discord ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the server address (IP)?

The official connection address is play.spysmp.live. Click the address box above to copy it to your clipboard.

Is this network pay-to-win?

Never. SpySMP is strictly committed to fair play. Ranks grant cosmetic prefixes, chat formatting, and convenience commands, but zero combat advantages or overpowered gear.

Which Minecraft editions and versions are supported?

Java Edition 1.20 through 1.21. Bedrock crossplay on port 19132 is actively supported.

What user data is stored when creating an account?

We practice extreme data minimization: we only store your chosen username, a cryptographically salted password hash (we cannot view your password), and your IP address for anti-abuse rate limiting. Read our Privacy Policy.

Can players under 13 join?

Anyone may freely play on the public Minecraft servers. However, registering a web account is restricted to ages 13 and older in strict compliance with COPPA. We do not collect under-13 user data.
